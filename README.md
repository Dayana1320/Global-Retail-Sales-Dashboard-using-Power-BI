# Global Retail Sales Dashboard using Power BI

## Project Overview

The **Global Retail Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The project analyzes global retail sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, regional performance, customer segments, product performance, discounts, returns, and shipping modes.

The main objective is to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights and support **data-driven decision-making**.

---

##  Business Problem

Global retail businesses generate large amounts of sales data across different regions, markets, products, customers, and shipping modes.

Analyzing this data manually can make it difficult to:

* Identify profitable and underperforming regions
* Understand sales and profit trends
* Identify top-performing products
* Analyze customer segments
* Understand the impact of discounts
* Monitor returned orders
* Evaluate shipping performance

This project addresses these challenges by creating an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Collection

Collected the Global Superstore retail dataset containing order, customer, product, sales, profit, shipping, and return information.

### 2. Data Cleaning

Used **Power Query** to:

* Remove unnecessary columns
* Handle missing values
* Correct data types
* Clean text fields
* Remove duplicate records where required
* Prepare data for analysis

### 3. Data Transformation

Transformed and prepared the data for reporting and visualization.

### 4. Data Modeling

Created relationships between the Orders, Returns, and People tables to build an effective Power BI data model.

### 5. DAX Measures

Created measures to calculate important business KPIs such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total quantity
* Top 10 products by sales
* Average shipping cost
* Profit Margin %
* Return Rate %

### 6. Dashboard Development

Created interactive visuals, KPI cards, slicers, charts, and drill-through functionality.

### 7. Business Analysis

Analyzed the dashboard to identify important trends, patterns, and business opportunities.

## Business Questions

The dashboard answers the following business questions:

1. Which regions have the highest and lowest profits?

2. Are discounts reducing profits on some orders?

3. Which customer segment — Consumer, Corporate, or Home Office — is most profitable?

4. How many orders are returned per region?

5. Which products generate the most revenue?

6. Which shipping modes are most cost-effective?

7. Are regional sales managers achieving balanced performance?

---

## Key Insights

* Regional sales and profitability vary significantly.
* Some regions generate high sales but lower profits.
* Discounts can negatively affect profitability on certain orders.
* Customer segments have different sales and profit contributions.
* Top-performing products contribute significantly to total revenue.
* Returned orders can affect overall business performance.
* Shipping modes have different cost and efficiency levels.
